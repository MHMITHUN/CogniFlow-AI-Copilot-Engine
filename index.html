1. The Executive Problem: The Enterprise LLM Trilemma

Enterprises deploying Large Language Models (such as OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini) face an existential trilemma between innovation speed and operational liability:

  • Customer Data Privacy (GDPR / PII Breach): When employees or customer service agents paste prompts containing client phone numbers (e.g. 01711-223344), account details, or National IDs, transmitting them unmasked to third-party model providers violates data sovereignty laws.
  • Hallucinations & Brand Liability: LLMs generate plausible-sounding falsehoods with high linguistic confidence (e.g. fabricating fake institutional partnerships or altered product warranties).
  • Unchecked Financial Actions: Giving AI agents autonomy to trigger API refunds or ledger payouts without human-in-the-loop sign-off creates severe corporate fiscal vulnerability.

3. Agile Product Roadmap (3 Sprints)

As Junior Product Manager, I organized the engineering sprints into sequential security layers to unblock marketing and support teams safely:

Sprint 1 • MVP
Proxy & PII Redaction
  • Low-latency reverse proxy for LLM API requests.
  • Regex & spaCy NER redactor for Bangladeshi phone & NID formats.
  • Encrypted session token vault for reversible unmasking.
Sprint 2 • Trust
Grounding & Fact-Checking
  • Grounding scorer cross-referencing company knowledge base.
  • Dynamic Amber UI highlighting for unverified claims.
  • Confidence percentage scoring algorithm.
Sprint 3 • Governance
Human Gates & SOC2 Audit
  • Human-in-the-Loop approval gate for financial refunds.
  • Enterprise Policy Configurator dashboard for PMs.
  • Immutable compliance audit trail with CSV export.
